Municipality of Paracale

Scholarship applications, verified and ranked in one place

Submit your registration form, signed school ID, barangay indigency and certificate of grade once. The scholarship office verifies each document, ranks applicants fairly, and publishes the decision to your own status page.

How it works

  1. 1
    Apply online

    Give your full name, email, address and school, then attach the four required documents.

  2. 2
    Document verification

    A verification officer checks every file and asks for a re-upload when a scan is unreadable.

  3. 3
    Ranking

    A weighted score combines academics, financial need, activities and the essay.

  4. 4
    Decision

    The committee approves, waitlists or declines. You see the result on your status page.
